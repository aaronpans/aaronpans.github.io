Author: Aaron Pan. Version 1.0. Published: 2026-09-08.

Decision: pass / conditional deployment / retraining required. This author-developed checklist is an organization-defined capability gate, not an accredited credential. Define critical failures, assessor authority, conditional limits and renewal triggers before use.
