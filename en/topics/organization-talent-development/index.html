Organization Capability & Talent Development

Scalable capability is designed into work—not stored in a few experts

The goal is to make experience identifiable, teachable, observable, certifiable and transferable as the organization grows.

Six-stage capability path

1. Define outcomes
Set the role's required operating results and quality floor.
2. Decompose tasks
Identify critical tasks, judgment points, common errors and interfaces.
3. Model capability
Translate knowledge, skill and judgment into observable requirements.
4. Train and coach
Use demonstration, practice, scenarios, feedback and workplace coaching.
5. Certify and deploy
Use performance evidence and explicit gates before independent work.
6. Verify at work
Review behavior stability, business outcomes and pipeline supply.
