Governance Playbook 02 · Closure

Store Inspection Closure Workflow

A finding is not closed when the checklist is submitted. It is closed only after accountable correction, field evidence, verification against an acceptance criterion and a recorded decision.

Risk firstDefine service times by risk. Unsafe or materially exposed conditions require immediate containment and escalation before the normal correction clock applies.

Eight-stage closure workflow

StageRequired actionMinimum evidenceExit criterion
1. Detect and classifyRecord the deviation and provisional risk level.Standard reference and observed condition.Finding and risk class recorded.
2. Capture evidenceDocument location, time and field context.Photo, record, reading or observation.Evidence is sufficient for another reviewer.
3. Assign owner and deadlineName one accountable owner.Acceptance and organization-defined due time.Ownership is acknowledged.
4. Contain immediate riskStop or isolate unacceptable exposure.Containment action and escalation record.Immediate risk is controlled.
5. Correct the deviationRestore the condition and address the immediate cause.Action record and field proof.Correction is ready for verification.
6. Verify effectivenessCheck the result against explicit criteria.Verifier, time, evidence and result.Pass, reject or escalate.
7. Close, reject or escalateRecord the decision and rationale.Closure decision and approver where required.System status matches the field condition.
8. Analyze recurrenceTrigger root-cause and preventive action when needed.Trend, cause and system-change decision.Prevention owner and follow-up are assigned.

Minimum record fields

  1. Store, location, timestamp and standard reference.
  2. Risk class, impact and escalation status.
  3. Finding description and original field evidence.
  4. Accountable owner, deadline and corrective action.
  5. Corrective evidence, verifier and verification result.
  6. Closure decision, recurrence flag and preventive-action link.

Closure acceptance test

Ask three questions: Is the required condition restored? Is there verifiable field evidence? Is recurrence risk acceptable or assigned to a preventive action? If any answer is no, the issue remains open, rejected or escalated.

BoundaryThis is a baseline management workflow, not a regulatory audit standard. Add legal, food-safety, labor, franchise and local reporting requirements applicable to the business.
