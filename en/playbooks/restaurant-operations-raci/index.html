Governance Playbook 01 · Responsibility

Restaurant Operations RACI Template

Use this baseline matrix to separate who defines, trains, executes, inspects, corrects and verifies. The primary control is simple: every activity needs one clearly accountable role.

Adapt before useRole names below are functional placeholders. Map them to your actual headquarters, regional, franchise and store structure; confirm authority and segregation-of-duty requirements.

Five controls before approval

  1. Each activity has one accountable role; any exception is explicitly justified.
  2. Responsible roles have the time, tools, authority and capability to perform.
  3. The person verifying closure is sufficiently independent from the corrective action.
  4. Franchisee, regional and store authority boundaries are explicit where applicable.
  5. Escalation, absence coverage and record ownership are defined outside the matrix.
Common failureA RACI is not complete when it only assigns letters. Each role must understand the decision, receive the required evidence and have authority to act.
