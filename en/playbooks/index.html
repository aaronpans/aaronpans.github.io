Restaurant Operations Governance Playbooks

Restaurant Operations Playbooks

These baseline templates answer three execution questions: who owns each step, what evidence closes an issue, and what proof allows a person to work independently.

Template boundaryThese are author-developed starting templates, not a universal organization chart, legal requirement, accredited certification or disclosure of any company's internal process. Adapt roles, authority, risk levels and service times before use.

Responsibility

Restaurant Operations RACI

Assign one accountable owner while separating definition, training, execution, inspection, correction and verification.

Closure

Store Inspection Closure Workflow

Move a finding from detection through ownership, correction, evidence, verification, closure and recurrence prevention.

Capability

Training Certification Checklist

Replace attendance-only completion with observed performance, an explicit decision and post-deployment verification.
