Governance Playbook 03 · Capability

Restaurant Training Certification Checklist

Certification should answer one operational question: can this person perform the defined task independently, under relevant conditions, with acceptable risk?

Credential boundary“Certification” here means an organization-defined capability gate. It is not an accredited credential, government license or industry-wide certification unless separately identified.

Twelve-item certification checklist

StageChecklist itemAcceptance evidence
Entry1. Role and task scope are defined.Authorized activities and boundaries are stated.
Entry2. Prerequisites are confirmed.Required knowledge, access and prior qualification are recorded.
Standard3. Outcomes, behaviors and conditions are observable.Assessment criteria contain no vague verbs without evidence.
Learning4. The complete standard is demonstrated.Learner observes correct sequence and critical controls.
Practice5. The learner practices under supervision.Practice record and coaching feedback exist.
Risk6. Critical errors and escalation rules are understood.Learner identifies stop conditions and escalation path.
Assessment7. An authorized assessor observes performance.Assessor identity, date, task and conditions are recorded.
Assessment8. Explicit pass criteria are applied.Critical items and tolerances are judged consistently.
Evidence9. Assessment proof is retained.Observation result, outputs and exceptions are traceable.
Decision10. The outcome is explicit.Pass, conditional deployment or retraining required.
Deployment11. Independent-work authority is stated.Permitted tasks, limits and supervision needs are clear.
Follow-up12. Verification and renewal triggers are scheduled.Field follow-up, expiry or retraining trigger is recorded.

Certification decision

Outcome 01

Pass

All critical criteria are met and independent deployment is authorized.

Outcome 02

Conditional deployment

Defined work is allowed with restrictions, supervision or a follow-up deadline.

Outcome 03

Retraining required

Critical criteria are not met; practice and reassessment are required before deployment.

Quality controls

  1. Assessors use the same version and interpretation of the standard.
  2. Critical failures cannot be averaged away by noncritical scores.
  3. Evidence is sufficient for a second reviewer to understand the decision.
  4. Conditional deployment has explicit limits, supervision and expiry.
  5. Standard changes, long inactivity or repeated errors trigger renewal.
Do not confuseCourse completion records exposure. This checklist verifies capability. Post-deployment evidence is still required to confirm that performance remains stable in real work.
